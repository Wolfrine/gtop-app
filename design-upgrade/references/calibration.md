# Benchmark calibration — criteria v1.1

9 October 2026. This addendum calibrates the [evaluation criteria](../evaluations/criteria.md) with observed reference qualities. The six visual weights, five journey weights, 1–10 scale and score-band meanings are unchanged. Version 1.0 is preserved at [criteria-v1.0.md](../evaluations/criteria-v1.0.md). No screen had been scored, so no rescoring is required.

## What the anchors mean in practice

| Dimension | Named quality to compare against | Evidence that can support an 8–9 judgment | What does not earn the score |
|---|---|---|---|
| Composition/hierarchy | DES’s typographic landmark; Brilliant’s proposition/demonstration relationship | A strong, task-appropriate focal order that survives actual copy, states and mobile composition | Merely enlarging a heading or adding empty space |
| Typography | twks’s scale/placement hierarchy; Primer’s row-label/value distinction | Intentional type roles, readable content and controlled wrapping at the actual density | Copying a fashionable font or tiny labels |
| Spacing/alignment/rhythm | twks’s editorial grid; Primer’s repeatable rows | Clear group relationships and consistent alignment with enough space for the task | A uniform large gap everywhere |
| Colour/tonal relationships | Linear’s surface steps; Bicycle’s semantic colour links | Emphasis and status meaning remain consistent and legible | A dark theme, gradient or saturated palette alone |
| Art direction/identity | DES’s related course imagery; Trust’s coherent drawn world | A recognizable, content-relevant visual language across neighboring GTOP screens | Decorative assets that do not belong to the learning context |
| Component finish/consistency | Primer’s sorting arrow/order feedback; twks’s active-filter chip/reset | Polished and predictable controls across required states and viewports | One attractive default card |
| Next action and feedback | Trust’s first choice followed by text and diagram consequence | The action, state change and meaning can be verified in the actual flow | Confetti or a success colour without useful explanation |
| Repeated use, navigation and recovery | Primer sort and twks filters provide bounded examples only | GTOP-specific retrieval, dense data, interruption and recovery observations | Assuming a public marketing page proves an admin journey |

These are **property anchors, not scores awarded to entire reference sites**. A reference can teach one exceptional quality while having an unresolved mobile, control or accessibility limitation.

- **1–4:** substantial incoherence or obstruction remains, with exact evidence.
- **5–6:** functional basics, but hierarchy, identity or finish remains generic or unresolved.
- **7:** the relevant relationships are coherent and competent; explain the remaining gap against a named suitable reference.
- **8:** deliberate craft holds across the required states and desktop/mobile composition. Name the equivalent reference quality and show its GTOP evidence.
- **9:** unusually resolved, distinctive and appropriate work with few meaningful weaknesses. Explain why it stands alongside the strongest relevant qualities, including repeated-use states.
- **10:** rare exemplar for the agreed purpose, requiring explicit justification and user agreement. This study establishes no whole-site 10/10 example.

## Rules for Step 4

Choose the benchmark by the task, then compare the same property. A practice screen can use DES as an identity reference, but requires Trust/Bicycle-style teaching evidence and GTOP's actual question states for a journey assessment. An admin screen can achieve strong identity through precision and coherence without cinematic imagery.

Use equivalent content density and comparable viewport sizes. Record the source capture path, region/state and reason for the comparison. Keep desktop and mobile distinct. Missing motion, focus, recovery or dense-state evidence remains `not observed`; do not use zero or fill the gap with an average. Scores remain design judgments, not human preference measurements.

No 8+ conclusion is justified by an award, a successful capture, an expensive effect or a working build. Keep serious functional and accessibility findings visible independently of an average. Actual user acceptance remains a separate gate.
