# GTOP Wiki Curation Ledger

This ledger records raw-to-wiki processing for GTOP product memory. It is operational bookkeeping, not product wiki content.

Use it to answer:

- Which `raw/` notes have been reviewed?
- Which wiki pages changed?
- Which durable decisions or open questions were captured?
- Which action items were surfaced for project management instead of stored as wiki todos?

## Rules

- Update this ledger during every `skills/wiki-curator` run.
- Keep entries concise and factual.
- Mark raw notes as `processed`, `skipped`, or `needs-follow-up`.
- Do not duplicate the full raw note or wiki content here.
- Do not use this ledger as a task tracker; use `Project Manager Handoff` for actionable execution items.
