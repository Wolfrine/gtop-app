## Observations

**Preserve:** Learner-only view prioritizes joining; admin view exposes scheduling and upcoming sessions.

**Finding (moderate, high confidence):** For admins, the lengthy host form stacks before join/upcoming on mobile. Greyed manual selection and other disabled controls dominate without enough nearby explanation of readiness.

**Improvement objective:** Separate quick joining from host setup and make prerequisites and scheduling state easy to understand.
