# Raw Note - 2026-09-06 - Plant Lab Experience Review

## Source

Founder feedback in the Plant Reproduction conversation, followed by a review of the current lesson source, interaction rules, layout and existing artwork.

## Context

The founder likes the overall direction but found it hard to know what to do next or how to try alternatives. He expects younger students exploring alone to give up and conclude that there is little to do. He requests assessment first, followed by thoughts and a plan for stronger design, art, guidance and a streamlined experience.

## Key Points

- Founder observation: acceptable overall direction does not establish intuitive independent use.
- Founder concern: unclear next actions and weak opportunities for experimentation may cause young learners to abandon the lab. This is a concern, not measured student abandonment.
- Source review: two initial questions and per-exploration prediction gates delay interaction. Several navigation and completion controls compete for attention.
- Source review: successful pollen transfer alone cannot complete its activity because a petal trial is also required. Navigating between explorations clears partial trial state. Closing the leaf example removes a completion prerequisite.
- Source review: several activities can be completed by advancing a sequence slider to its endpoint. Alternative trials have little persistent visual comparison. The field notebook largely repeats authored explanations.
- Source review: the rich garden background and simple interactive diagrams have different visual character. Some biological developments are opacity changes, limiting visible response and discovery.
- Review limit: the live browser reached the signed-out landing page; the local preview could not connect. This turn did not establish a signed-in visual or mobile walkthrough.
- Agent proposal, not approved design: a connected flower-to-new-plant journey, immediate object interaction, contextual one-step guidance, meaningful alternative trials, persistent observations and coordinated art and scientific motion.

## Decisions

- The current requested output is assessment and proposed direction. Redesign implementation has not been requested in this review turn.
- Preserve the founder's expectation that a child should discover the next action and worthwhile experiments without adult explanation.
- The previously cancelled feedback automation remains outside this work.
