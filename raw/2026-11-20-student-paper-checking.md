# Individual student papers in GT Papers

## Source

Owner’s GT Papers workflow decision, 20 November 2026.

## Context

Students send staff scanned answer PDFs through the channels they already use. A teacher may receive one answer book at a time, independently of a class collection.

## Decision

The teacher selects the student’s existing GTOP account after the student’s first sign-in, attaches that student’s PDF and checks it as one answer book. Staff can return to the student’s history to review or print an approved copy. Checking a class stack remains available when several books arrive together.
