## Observations

**Preserve:** Saved/unsaved status, answers retained on failed save, useful incorrect-only filtering and answer explanations.

**Finding (moderate, high confidence):** The large mobile doughnut, title and three stacked actions dominate before answer review. The invitation-save offer appears after the answer list; the successful save/retry behavior should be retained independently of this visual reprioritization.

**Improvement objective:** Make the reasoning to revisit and the next learning action prominent, keeping score and storage status clear but proportionate.
