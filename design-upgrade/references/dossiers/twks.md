# twks

[Live source](https://twks.ch/en) · Inspected 9 October 2026 · Agency and project discovery

**Aesthetic description:** Editorial, image-led, confident. This is evaluator interpretation, not a measured human response.

## First impression

The live hero gives imagery the entire field and lays a quiet navigation line across it. Below it, emphatic text and an asymmetric case grid carry the identity with very little interface ornament.

## Aesthetic profile

| Aspect | Observed treatment |
|---|---|
| Composition | A large indented introduction contrasts with narrow descriptive columns; case imagery alternates size and placement. |
| Typography | A small range of sans-serif treatments creates hierarchy through scale, line breaks and placement, with fine metadata kept secondary. |
| Colour and material | The shell stays black and white while each project supplies its own colour and photographic or graphic language. |
| Rhythm and density | Large intervals give projects individual presence. The projects listing has a stable three-column desktop grid. |
| Motion | Hero media changes over time in live inspection. The project filter visibly replaces the displayed set and exposes an active filter with Reset. |
| Identity | Consistent editorial rules contain very diverse project images; coherence comes from the frame around the work. |

## What makes it useful

Strong content hierarchy and a restrained shell let distinctive subject imagery do the expressive work. Filter state is visible and reversible.

## Behavior actually inspected

Opened Projects, then Services, then Digital. The resulting list showed a Digital chip, an active-filter count and Reset. The selected before/after captures are in the gallery.

## Limits and what not to transfer

The CI normal-motion hero was often black and some case media did not load. Those regions are not aesthetic benchmarks; the loaded live hero and inspected project grid provide separate evidence. Small grey controls and large gaps are not a complete model for dense admin use. No exact contrast or performance audit was conducted.

## GTOP application hypothesis

Consider a quiet shared shell around expressive lab/topic imagery. In question banks and reports, make selected filters explicit and provide an obvious reset. Do not carry the sparse agency pacing into everyday admin lists.
