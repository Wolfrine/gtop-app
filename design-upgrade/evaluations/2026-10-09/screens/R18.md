## Observations

**Preserve:** Strong typographic landmark, related educational subject imagery, tonal section rhythm and two real entry demonstrations.

**Finding (moderate, medium confidence):** The page has more deliberate identity than the app shell. The g/star mark changes to three bars after entry. The mobile full-page archive inconsistently paints lower subject images despite loaded-image metadata; normal-scroll completeness is not established by these stills.

**Improvement objective:** Preserve the distinctive learning invitation and demonstration; make the handoff to the signed-in product feel like one brand and verify the full mobile scroll sequence.

**Evidence limit / follow-up:** Desktop 8-level composition/identity dimensions are supported by the hero and related section sequence, not a whole-site 8+ rating. Mobile lower imagery/reveal behavior and normal-motion timing remain unverified; no missing-asset production bug is asserted.
