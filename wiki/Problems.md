# Problems

## Summary

GTOP should focus on product problems that affect practice quality, learning continuity, admin confidence, and future Growth Tutorials ecosystem integration.

## Student Practice Friction

Students need a simple path from login to useful practice. Any flow that makes them choose too much too early, lose context after a quiz, or struggle to retry weak areas weakens GTOP's core learning value.

The practice loop should support quick start, focused customization, useful summaries, and repeatable retry behavior.

## Learning Feedback Clarity

Quiz results should help students understand what happened and what to do next. Scores alone are not enough. GTOP needs to preserve the link between attempts, missed questions, reports, and retry choices so students can improve over time.

## Content Quality And Trust

Admins need confidence that MCQs are correct, syllabus-aligned, discoverable, and maintainable. Question flagging, syllabus counts, and admin tools all support this problem.

If the question bank becomes difficult to trust or maintain, the student-facing product loses credibility.

## Live Practice Energy

Live Quiz and Quiz Run modes are intended to make practice feel more social, competitive, and timely. The product problem is not only real-time mechanics; it is whether live practice increases engagement without confusing the core quiz and reports experience.

## Ecosystem Boundary Clarity

GTOP should stay independent while supporting Hub return paths and ecosystem integration. Future work must avoid making GTOP feel like a hidden submodule of another product.
