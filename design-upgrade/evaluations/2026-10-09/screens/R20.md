## Observations

**Preserve:** Account context, editable curriculum and visible save confirmation.

**Finding (moderate, high confidence):** Eight equally styled metadata tiles receive more space than the two editable fields. On mobile the tiles form a long stack before the actual profile task; admin controls inherit that separation.

**Improvement objective:** Separate editable learning context from secondary account/activity facts and keep save feedback near the fields.
