## Observations

**Preserve:** A focused question card and visibly selected answer; no decoration competes with the question.

**Finding (moderate, high confidence):** The long Solutions question exposes the mobile cost of the motto, progress, curriculum metadata and repeated question numbering before the answer set. The final action falls beyond the opening view.

**Improvement objective:** Compose around realistic long questions and answers; keep progress and the next action easy to locate.

**Evidence limit / follow-up:** The successful invited completion uses an admin fixture permitted across curricula. Production sign-in and a matching-curriculum learner handoff were not exercised.
