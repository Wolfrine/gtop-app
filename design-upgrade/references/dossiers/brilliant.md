# Brilliant

[Live source](https://brilliant.org/) · Inspected 9 October 2026 · Public learning proposition

**Aesthetic description:** Warm, encouraging, clear. This is evaluator interpretation, not a measured human response.

## First impression

A warm background, emphatic serif headline, green action and friendly illustrated teaching demo make the educational proposition feel approachable. The mobile layout changes the order and emphasis of hero material.

## Aesthetic profile

| Aspect | Observed treatment |
|---|---|
| Composition | Desktop pairs the learning proposition with a demonstration; mobile places the visual prominently above supporting content. |
| Typography | Serif display text adds character while sans-serif controls and explanations remain direct. |
| Colour and material | Warm neutrals, green emphasis, pastel teaching panels and soft corners create a controlled friendly vocabulary. |
| Rhythm and density | Alternating explanation and demonstration sections keep the public page easy to browse; this is not evidence about paid lesson pacing. |
| Motion | The hero contains an animated demonstration with a Pause label exposed in the DOM; activation was not verified. A cursor inside that demonstration is part of the media, not evidence of the reviewer manipulating the lesson. |
| Identity | Avatar, illustrations, surface colours and typography repeat a coherent educational tone. |

## What makes it useful

The visual language gives subject matter personality while keeping the main proposition and action understandable. The demonstration’s motion control remains unverified; it is excluded as a positive interaction benchmark.

## Behavior actually inspected

Generic page scroll and normal/reduced preference capture, followed by verified banner dismissal. Pause activation remained unresolved and the subject-tab action was not reached. See the interaction register.

## Limits and what not to transfer

The initial mobile cookie banner obscured part of the page, so a supplemental pass dismissed the banner and preserved a verified clean entry. Pause and hover activation attempts timed out; no pause/resume or subject-tab result is claimed. Only public content was inspected; no account, paid lesson, learning effectiveness or student preference is inferred. The current homepage emphasizes Koji; older screenshots should not be substituted for this observed version.

## GTOP application hypothesis

For public entry and learner introductions, pair a direct promise with relevant educational imagery and a clear primary action. For demonstrations, expose pause/resume and keep the essential explanation available independently of playback.
