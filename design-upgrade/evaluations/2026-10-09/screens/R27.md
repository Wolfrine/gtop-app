## Observations

**Preserve:** Explicit distinction between draft inventory and verified syllabus; source/completeness criteria and approval safeguards.

**Finding (major, high confidence):** The unfiltered fixture lists 273 subjects, yielding a 24,107px desktop and 54,774px mobile page. The selected subject editor is appended after that inventory (56,736px mobile with an unsaved edit). Filters exist, but the default structure separates selection from editing substantially.

**Improvement objective:** Keep the active subject and its evidence near the inventory context; make large-volume review practical without losing verification safeguards.

**Evidence limit / follow-up:** No claim is made about runtime speed or production subject counts. Preview and unsaved notes were exercised; approval and save outcomes were not.
