# Raw Note - 2026-10-24 - Classroom copy and assistant feedback

## Source

Sachit's direct request in the GTOP local bot conversation on 24 October 2026.

## Context

The working Question Paper assistant produced printable documents containing internal caveats, and conversation replies felt long, confusing and resistant to straightforward requests. Existing papers were difficult for administrators to discover across conversations.

## Key Points

- A generated paper will be printed as-is and handed to students. Do not expect teachers to remove statements about GTOP syllabus, non-official formats, generation processes or internal review afterward. Deliver a finished examination document with separate usable answers.
- The assistant should speak briefly, warmly and naturally. A simple yes, greeting or acknowledgement should not trigger a long capability explanation. Preserve details already provided and answer the actual question.
- Review actual conversations daily, share a concise summary and confirm a specific correction plan with Sachit before applying future changes.
- Administrators should see papers and their conversations across creators, organized by board, standard and subject, to refer to existing work and reduce unnecessary repeat generation.
- Name conversations meaningfully from the paper produced, rather than retaining the first greeting or a truncated request forever.

## Decisions

The classroom-copy correction is the first release. The previously deferred paper library is now expressly approved. Administrative source trust and approval information remain important but belong outside the student copy. Daily monitoring does not grant autonomous permission to change the assistant.
