## Observations

**Preserve:** Predict → experiment → check sequence; circuit and numerical readings respond to real slider/arrangement input.

**Finding (moderate, high confidence):** The desktop experiment places controls beside the circuit. Mobile stacks progress, prediction, diagram, readings and controls; the learner must scroll to connect several parts. The full five-question check/feedback is especially long.

**Improvement objective:** Strengthen proximity between the changed variable and its consequence, while preserving predictions and explanatory feedback.

**Evidence limit / follow-up:** Keyboard slider changes were observed in Chromium. A touch-drag, physical phone, saved-attempt reload and motion/performance study were not conducted.
