## Observations

**Preserve:** Four explicit stages, preserved format context, coverage check before preview, separate answer key.

**Finding (moderate, high confidence):** The stage sequence works in the captured minimal plan. Global paper navigation, back/saved links and stage navigation accumulate before the task on mobile; the three format choices wrap as two plus one.

**Improvement objective:** Make the current stage dominant and the plan summary easy to revisit; keep coverage and preview consequences explicit.

**Evidence limit / follow-up:** One-question local preview reached. Draft saving/resuming, shortages and complex multi-section recovery were not exercised.
