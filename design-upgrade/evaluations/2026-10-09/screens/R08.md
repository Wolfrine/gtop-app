## Observations

**Preserve:** Distinct queued, needs-reply, failed, cancelled and ready states; saved papers are explicitly unchanged by a new request.

**Finding (moderate, high confidence):** Long titles and repeated readiness labels consume much of the mobile opening. The captured needs-info state asks for an answer while pointing elsewhere for the actual question; fixture completeness limits that finding.

**Improvement objective:** Prioritize the current request, exact needed response and next action; keep prior activity available without duplicating the summary.

**Evidence limit / follow-up:** Status variants are seeded. Revision editor/history were opened; actual reply, retry, cancellation, scheduling and eventual completion were not exercised.
