**Desktop 6.8/10 · Mobile 6.4/10** — visual craft, rubric 1.1. Evaluated 9 October 2026; awaiting user review.

Route `/dashboard` · inventory roles: user, admin. Task: learner dashboard. Scope includes the captured entry and variants below. These are judgments of the captured design, not human preference measurements.

[All scores](../README.md) · [Method and baseline](../method.md) · [Journey evidence](../journeys.md)

## Observations

**Preserve:** Clear quick/custom practice hierarchy, recent work, progress and an explicit invited-practice return card.

**Finding (moderate, high confidence):** The generic motivational hero is much taller on mobile than the useful return/progress context. The invitation-return variant already places a specific next step above the hero, which is a useful precedent.

**Improvement objective:** Give returning learners a relevant next action and recent learning context, while keeping warmth and quick practice.
