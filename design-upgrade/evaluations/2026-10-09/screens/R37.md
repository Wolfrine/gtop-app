## Observations

**Preserve:** Saved attempt access, curriculum context, trend and retry actions.

**Finding (moderate, high confidence):** Aggregate metric cards and quiz-type distribution precede the actual practice history. Each mobile attempt repeats several score representations and two large retry actions, creating a 5,838px captured page from a small history.

**Improvement objective:** Help a learner identify what to revisit and find prior work quickly; reduce repeated information per attempt.

**Evidence limit / follow-up:** Opening a saved attempt was observed. Search/filtering at larger history volume and successful retry execution are not established.
