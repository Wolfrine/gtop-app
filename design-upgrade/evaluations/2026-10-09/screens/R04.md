## Observations

**Preserve:** Restrained green document workspace, saved/unchanged status, separate student paper and teacher key.

**Finding (moderate, high confidence):** Desktop gives the document a clear frame. Mobile repeats the long title and version/context metadata before the paper body, which starts low on the page. The missing-paper state has an explicit explanation and retry.

**Improvement objective:** Keep document identity, version and print action clear while making the actual paper easier to inspect on a phone.

**Evidence limit / follow-up:** Answer-key switching was observed. Native print/save-PDF output was not validated.
