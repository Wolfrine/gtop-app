## Observations

**Preserve:** Concept diagnosis, honest distinction between attempts and learners, feedback and saved-attempt context.

**Finding (moderate, high confidence):** Mobile gives high space priority to navigation, explanatory text and aggregate counts; useful concept comparisons begin later. The distinction between practice completion and retained results needs to stay visible.

**Improvement objective:** Make weak concepts and their supporting evidence easy to scan before detailed feedback and attempt records.
