## Observations

**Preserve:** Focused question/answer surface, selected state, progression and explicit empty-quiz message.

**Finding (moderate, high confidence):** Repeated question numbering and the motto consume space without helping the answer task. Run/live variants add timer and submission context; these need a consistent place that does not displace long question content.

**Improvement objective:** Build a stable reading-and-answering composition across ordinary, invited and timed practice.
