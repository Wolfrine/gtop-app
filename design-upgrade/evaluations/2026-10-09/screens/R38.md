## Observations

**Preserve:** Curriculum filters and an in-place edit state.

**Finding (major, high confidence):** The desktop header/row order is hard to associate; there is no visible page heading. Mobile stacks header labels separately from tall cards of unlabeled values, with the edit icon isolated above them.

**Improvement objective:** Keep board, class, subject and chapter explicitly associated with each editable record and provide clear page/context navigation.
