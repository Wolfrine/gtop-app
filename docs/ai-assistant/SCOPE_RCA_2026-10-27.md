# Requirement continuity correction — 27 October 2026

## Evidence and causes

The bounded review window was 25 October 18:51:33 to 26 October 18:51:33 UTC. Three real administrator conversations received delayed replies; three turn jobs completed, no paper/revision was created, and no feedback was submitted. Two requests predated the window. Replies were 5–29 words; this small sample did not show a new verbosity trend.

1. **Scope contamination:** a conversation changed from Class 11 IB Mathematics to Class 9 ICSE Biology. `extractScope` correctly deleted incompatible qualifiers, but `buildTaskBrief` used `Object.assign`, which did not remove the old `programme: IBDP`. Its source reference also survived. `verifiedRequirements` could additionally reintroduce removed fields by spreading an older active paper before the brief. Curriculum resolution then rejected the irrelevant programme metadata. The turn was marked completed because it delivered a clarification; this was not successful paper creation.
2. **Lost chapter scope:** both recorded local-model proposals omitted `selection`. Deterministic extraction only handled forms such as “Chapters: …” and “… are the chapters.” The actual comma/period-separated request named Digestive system and 40 marks. The brief fell back to full syllabus instead of retaining that chapter. The second turn stopped at an exact-pattern clarification before any paper job existed.
3. **A genuine data constraint:** live bounded syllabus reads for ICSE Class 9 returned no entries, including Biology; the exact published Biology release document was absent. The retained worker lookup independently reported no saved chapters. Fixing stale metadata alone cannot supply a verified curriculum. An online check must remain explicit and may still need textbook/source clarification.
4. **Availability and latency are separate:** these turns waited approximately 13–18 hours while the listener was unavailable. The subsequent local inference metrics were 51.5, 73.1 and 89.7 seconds, with 47–51 seconds of model loading. These are historical observations, not a benchmark. The separately delivered outbound starter was publishing a fresh ready heartbeat during this RCA; the idle model remained unloaded. No additional always-warm policy is introduced.

Private transcripts, raw model replies and job identifiers remain in private runtime evidence. The examples in regression tests are educational scope fixtures without account information.

## Correction

- Replace curriculum scope fields with the extractor's full result, including deletions and source cleanup. Do not merge an obsolete paper over the resulting brief. A new creation request that changes curriculum becomes that task's root; earlier requests remain verbatim in history.
- Version task briefs to 2. Reconstruct older briefs from saved user evidence on their next normal turn, without bulk database edits or historical transcript changes. Retrieve at most 30 referenced evidence messages beyond the normal recent-message window; missing evidence fails closed.
- Recognize plainly named comma/period-separated chapters independently of optional model extraction. Store their exact user evidence. This records intent, not proof of curriculum availability: the gateway still rejects unknown chapter titles, unsupported widening, invalid sources and out-of-scope paper content.
- Offer a short, actionable curriculum check instead of exposing internal metadata errors. A positive follow-up starts only a curriculum check when the gateway has independently recorded curriculum unavailability. A model-written offer alone grants nothing. Holds, negations and ordinary conversation cannot approve a task.
- Keep the practice-format limitation and whole-question OR rule explicit, using a shorter clarification. An exact board format is not silently substituted.
