# Question paper builder · review direction

Level: L1–L2 admin workflow. The operator is a teacher/admin preparing a paper from approved questions. The screen should answer, in order: what format am I making, what syllabus does it cover, do I have enough approved questions, and what exactly will students receive?

## Direction
- Format choice is an index of the eight stored paper families plus custom objective. Each choice shows a representative question and a separate model answer toggle.
- The examples are illustrations in application code. They are never written to Firestore, counted as bank coverage, or printed as real papers.
- Stored descriptive templates receive an editable, mark-balanced section plan where their Firestore definition omitted counts. Explicitly counted templates remain authoritative.
- The specimen uses a quiet paper surface, precise marks and curriculum context. The rest of the workflow uses the existing GTOP shell and controls.
- Coverage and preview continue to use only approved questions and show shortages honestly. A saved draft retains its question and answer snapshots.

## Visual review checklist
Desktop and phone: scan format choices; select a written format; reveal its model answer; choose subject/chapter; inspect section counts, shortage messaging, paper preview and separate answer key; ensure no horizontal overflow. Repeat with a complete objective paper and a saved draft. The founder reviews the dev build before production.

## Pattern continuity
Board/class/subject changes retain the selected paper family and edited section rules. A scoped template mismatch is shown explicitly and blocks creation until resolved. Total marks stays editable: resizing preserves every section, type requirement and per-question mark value; impossible totals leave the previous plan intact with an explanation. Cover this transition in the browser regression alongside competency papers at 80, 41 and too-small totals.
