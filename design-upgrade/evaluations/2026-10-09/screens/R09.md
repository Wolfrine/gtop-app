## Observations

**Preserve:** Curriculum scope, question-type totals, readable questions and expandable answer details.

**Finding (major, high confidence):** The mobile view stacks multiple navigation tiers and curriculum controls before the first question. In archive confirmation, “selected” and “Cancel” split inside narrow controls. Empty search leaves a mostly blank list area with only a small zero count.

**Improvement objective:** Keep the working question and selected scope central; give selection/confirmation enough width and make empty-search recovery explicit.
