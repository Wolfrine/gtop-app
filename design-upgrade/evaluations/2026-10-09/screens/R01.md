## Observations

**Preserve:** Clear five-question scope, curriculum labels and honest explanation of solo practice.

**Finding (moderate, high confidence):** The mobile preview spends most of the opening view on scope and explanatory copy. Invalid and mismatched links have distinct messages and a dashboard exit; this useful specificity should survive.

**Improvement objective:** Make the invitation scope and eligible next action immediately recognizable, with secondary explanation subordinate.
