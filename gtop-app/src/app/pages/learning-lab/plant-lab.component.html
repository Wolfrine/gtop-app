Other ways to travel

Coconuts float on water. Hooked fruits such as Xanthium attach to animals. Balsam fruits split and scatter seeds. Dispersal is movement; germination is the later start of seed growth under suitable conditions.

A leaf can carry new beginnings

Buds along the leaf margin develop into plantlets. On suitable moist soil they can establish new plants. No pollen transfer or gamete fusion is involved.
