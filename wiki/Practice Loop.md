# Practice Loop

## Definition

The practice loop is GTOP's core learner journey: set learning context, choose relevant MCQ practice, answer questions, review the outcome, inspect reports, retry weak areas, and leave with a clearer next practice action.

It is a loop, not a single quiz event. A quiz attempt starts the evidence trail; summary, report, review, retry, and future recommendations convert that attempt into learning continuity.

## Why It Matters

Students already invest effort in preparation. GTOP should reduce the distance between effort and useful feedback: did the student practice the right area, what went wrong, what should be repeated, and what should happen next?

The loop is also GTOP's main source of product intelligence. Without repeated practice, [[Learning Diagnosis]] has little evidence and [[Student Pulse]] risks becoming a decorative report.

## Current GTOP State

GTOP already supports the practical shape of the loop through board and standard profile context, quick quizzes, custom quizzes, quiz summaries, reports, retry options, question review, and question flagging. These are not isolated features; together they create the basic learner rhythm.

[[Live Practice]] can strengthen the loop by creating urgency, social participation, and more attempt evidence. Its value is highest when live results still lead students back into review, reports, and retry behavior.

Example snapshot: a Class 10 student chooses a chapter quiz, misses several questions from the same concept, reviews the explanations, flags one confusing item, retries a weak subset, and leaves knowing which chapter area to practice next. The product memory in this loop is not only the score; it is the sequence from attempt to review to next action.

## Boundaries And Non-Goals

The practice loop should not become a maze of modes. Quick practice, custom practice, live events, and quiz runs may have different entry points, but they should preserve a recognizable finish: review what happened and know the next useful action.

The loop should not claim deep personalization from sparse activity. Early feedback can be simple and useful; stronger guidance needs enough attempts and enough trusted question metadata.

## Relationship To Other Concepts

The loop operationalizes [[Product Direction]]. [[Question Bank Quality]] determines whether the questions inside the loop are trustworthy. [[Learning Diagnosis]] interprets loop evidence. [[Student Pulse]] may summarize the loop over time. [[Live Practice]] adds energy but should not break continuity.

## Decision Guidance

Future features should be judged by whether they make the loop easier to start, easier to repeat, or easier to learn from. If a feature adds engagement but weakens review, retry, or diagnosis, it should be redesigned before becoming central.
