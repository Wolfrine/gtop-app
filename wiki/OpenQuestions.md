# Open Questions

## Summary

These questions should shape future GTOP product planning and wiki updates.

## Institute Admin Definition

What should an Institute Admin do in GTOP, and how is that role different from the current admin/teacher role?

## Student Progress Model

Should GTOP define a stronger product model for mastery, weak areas, streaks, assignments, or recommended next practice beyond current reports and retries?

## Live Quiz Versus Quiz Run Positioning

How should the product explain the difference between hosted Live Quiz events and peer-oriented Quiz Runs so students and admins choose the right mode?

## Admin Content Quality Workflow

What should happen after students flag questions? GTOP has the signal, but the ideal review workflow, prioritization, and resolution states need product definition.

## Growth Tutorials Ecosystem Relationship

How much should GTOP expose Hub return paths, Growth Tutorials branding, or cross-product navigation without weakening its independence as a standalone product?
