# Design Education Series

[Live source](https://des.obys.agency/) · Inspected 9 October 2026 · Expressive education storefront

**Aesthetic description:** Sculptural, editorial, deliberately spacious. This is evaluator interpretation, not a measured human response.

## First impression

A very large black DES mark dominates a pale field. Navigation and course metadata sit at a much smaller scale; the contrast makes the identity unmistakable, but the immediate learning action is less prominent.

## Aesthetic profile

| Aspect | Observed treatment |
|---|---|
| Composition | A strong vertical division, oversized letterforms, thin horizontal rules and large empty intervals establish a deliberate grid. |
| Typography | The identity is an active compositional object. Small metadata stays systematic while titles become the major landmarks. |
| Colour and material | Near-monochrome architecture unifies stone, fabric and sculptural course artwork. Colour is reserved for individual objects rather than repeated interface chrome. |
| Rhythm and density | The page alternates broad quiet space with large course imagery. This is a browsing rhythm, not the density of a daily practice workspace. |
| Motion | The recorded entrance and scroll reveal change the spatial composition. Course imagery is staged as a sequence; exact easing and performance are not established. |
| Identity | The same geometry, material treatment and type relationships recur across the inspected homepage. Creator intent connects the identity to organizing fragmented knowledge. |

## What makes it useful

Distinctive typography, consistent art direction and related course imagery make the page recognizable without a large collection of decorative components.

## Behavior actually inspected

Desktop slow/fast/reverse scroll observed in the recording; mobile touch follow-up in the interaction register. Private courses and checkout were not entered.

## Limits and what not to transfer

The generic mobile wheel sequence did not move the page; its initial mobile frames only establish the entry composition. A touch-specific follow-up successfully reached the introduction and course imagery; see the interaction register. Desktop Home/scrollTo did not reliably return the custom-scrolling page to its initial state. Neither observation is proof that ordinary user scrolling is broken. Small metadata and expansive gaps require a different treatment in time-sensitive GTOP tasks.

## GTOP application hypothesis

Use the principle of a recognizable typographic hierarchy and subject-specific visual language for the public entry and lab introductions. Preserve compact, direct controls inside practice. Do not adopt the identity, assets, long staging or extreme empty space.
