## What makes it useful

The key reference is correspondence: explanation, control, object and outcome belong to the same concept. Mobile retains a readable column and a wide control beneath the illustration.

## Behavior actually inspected

Generic scroll inspected the rider and first force examples. Final supplemental captures verify right/left movement of the first slider in both viewports, with visible knob and rider-orientation changes. The unsuccessful first attempt remains diagnostic.

## Limits and what not to transfer

The cloud browser could not initialize this WebGL scene; isolated Chromium rendered it successfully. This is an environment limitation, not proof of a site defect. Software-rendered recordings are not performance measurements. This 2023 article is a teaching-mechanism reference, not a current-fashion claim. No learning benefit was measured.

## GTOP application hypothesis

For plant and electrical/current labs, align the control with the visible mechanism and explain the resulting change nearby. Preserve causal meaning when motion is reduced. Avoid making 3D a requirement when a clearer 2D representation suffices.
