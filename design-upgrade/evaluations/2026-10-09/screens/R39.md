## Observations

**Preserve:** Recognizable account summaries, online/last-seen labels and expandable details.

**Finding (moderate, high confidence):** Eighteen profile cards are shown without a visible search control. Opening one desktop card stretches its grid row; mobile becomes a long roster. Some synthetic users display empty/broken-looking avatar circles; this is a fallback-state concern, not proof of broken production photos.

**Improvement objective:** Support finding a person, comparing key account context and opening details without disrupting the roster.

**Evidence limit / follow-up:** One card expansion observed; large-volume search, account changes and production avatar sources were not tested.
