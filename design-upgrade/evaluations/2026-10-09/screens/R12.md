## Observations

**Preserve:** Loaded-vs-awaiting totals, explicit selected count, flagged questions excluded from approval, individual editor and reason-bearing rejection confirmation.

**Finding (moderate, high confidence):** Desktop starts close to the drawer edge compared with neighboring pages. Mobile navigation and batch controls delay the list. Fifty loaded rows are lengthy, but pagination and a persistent bulk-action bar exist; this is not an absent-pagination finding.

**Improvement objective:** Reduce navigation overhead and tighten row comparison while retaining visible eligibility, selection scope and review reasons.
