## Observations

**Preserve:** Actionable status language, needs-action/ready filters, current ownership context, search and older-record loading.

**Finding (moderate, high confidence):** Desktop rows are orderly; mobile records remain readable but the page header, schedule and 2×2 tabs precede the first record. Loading older records extends an already long list; status and next action could be compared faster.

**Improvement objective:** Make urgent work and retrieval prominent, with a compact recurring record pattern and clear loaded-range context.
