# Raw Note - 2026-09-28 - Current Syllabus And Paper Coverage

## Source

Founder discussion in ChatGPT Work, 28 September 2026.

## Context

The founder asked why an add-content option had disappeared after recent GTOP work and what had changed from the stable design. The new question-paper flow also could not express chapters absent from the existing syllabus bank.

## Key Points

- A paper request should describe the intended board, standard, subject and chapters even when GTOP has no questions for that scope.
- For a full-syllabus request, the creator should consult the selected board's current official curriculum rather than infer completeness from existing questions.
- The founder explicitly extended the scope to complete current-year syllabus coverage across boards, standards, subjects and chapters, with preview and administrator verification before approval.
- The founder authorized the corrections and improvements. This authorizes implementation; it does not constitute an administrator's verification of imported syllabus content.

## Decisions

- Maintain current-year curriculum as a separately reviewable source of scope for papers and question-bank enrichment.
- Provide source-backed previews and admin approval before a curriculum is treated as verified.
- Preserve useful stable-design features and restore direct access to content creation.
- Missing chapters remain expressible and visible as gaps instead of silently reducing a full-syllabus request to available bank coverage.

## Open Questions

- Exact board/programme coverage, language or medium variants, and cohort/examination-year mapping must be visible where an official source differs from an academic-year label.
- Source availability and human verification determine when a particular subject can truthfully be called complete. The request for comprehensive coverage does not establish that the data already exists.
