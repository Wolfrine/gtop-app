# Product Direction

## Definition

GTOP's product direction is to become a practice-intelligence product for MCQ learning. It should connect trusted questions, repeated student practice, learning diagnosis, and restrained AI-assisted feedback so a student can answer the practical question: what should I practice next?

This direction is different from being only a quiz app, a content repository, or an AI wrapper. GTOP remains grounded in structured board, standard, subject, chapter, and MCQ discipline. Intelligence is valuable only when it makes that structure more useful for learners and Growth Tutorials operators.

## Why It Matters

The founder-level urgency is that GTOP cannot stop at "we have built a feature, now market it." The product has to reason from what students already receive, what the current question bank can support, what AI makes possible, and where education behavior is moving. That makes [[Question Bank Quality]], student usage, [[Learning Diagnosis]], and future-facing feedback strategic anchors rather than side improvements.

For Growth Tutorials, this direction protects brand trust. A large MCQ surface is only credible if students repeatedly find relevant questions, understand mistakes, and see that the product is learning enough from their attempts to guide the next step.

## Current GTOP State

The current product already has the raw material for this direction: student profile context, quick and custom quizzes, reports, retry flows, flagged-question signals, live participation, and admin content management. Public-facing product language already claims board-aligned scale, refreshed content, personalized analytics, AI-powered hints, and knowing what to revise next.

The wiki preserves a key tension: public copy references 50,000+ MCQs, while product discussion references roughly 1.2 lakh questions needing verification. GTOP should treat scale as something to validate and earn, not as a number that automatically creates trust.

Today, GTOP should be understood as structured MCQ practice with early reporting and review signals. The future direction is practice intelligence: the same practice activity should gradually support trustworthy diagnosis, next-practice guidance, content-quality feedback, and restrained AI assistance.

## Boundaries And Non-Goals

GTOP should not add AI as decoration, generic chat, or overconfident personalization. AI belongs where it strengthens the product's learning loop: question classification, topic tagging, weak-area explanation, content review, and concise student feedback.

The wiki should not turn this direction into a roadmap or implementation plan. Product execution, ownership, scheduling, branch work, and dashboard tracking belong with the project manager agent.

## Relationship To Other Concepts

[[Question Bank Quality]] is the base. [[Practice Loop]] creates repeated behavior. [[Learning Diagnosis]] interprets attempt evidence. [[Student Pulse]] is a possible learner-facing expression of diagnosis. [[Live Practice]] adds energy and usage signal, but it should feed the same review, report, retry, and diagnosis model instead of creating a separate product personality.

## Decision Guidance

Future product choices should be tested against a simple question: does this make structured practice more trustworthy, more repeatable, or easier to understand? If not, it is probably outside the current direction.

When data is thin, GTOP should show simpler review guidance instead of pretending to know the learner deeply. When content quality is uncertain, diagnosis and AI feedback should stay conservative.
