## Observations

**Preserve:** Clear disclosure that embedded app actions affect the signed-in account, with explicit viewport choices.

**Finding (minor, high confidence):** The mobile introduction and preview configuration dominate before the component examples. As an internal utility, it needs consistency and clear controls rather than showcase art direction.

**Improvement objective:** Keep preview choice and action compact; ensure component examples later cover actual approved screen states.

**Evidence limit / follow-up:** Internal/admin utility. Opening the responsive embedded app was not exercised in the evaluation evidence.
