# Findings and design priorities

9 October 2026 · [All ratings](README.md) · [Screen evidence](screens/R01.md) · [Journey boundaries](journeys.md)

The visual baseline is uneven, rather than uniformly weak. The landing scores **7.9 desktop / 7.4 mobile** and Plant Lab **7.7 / 7.1**. The current paper workspace is around **7.0 / 6.5–6.6**. The weakest confirmed mobile compositions are Live Host **3.4** and the syllabus editor **3.7**. These numbers summarize evaluator judgments; exact observations and dimension scores matter more than small numerical differences.

## Confirmed captured-state issues to address in the affected designs

| Finding | Exact evidence and observation | Consequence | Severity / confidence | Improvement objective |

| F03 · Narrow destructive confirmation | [R09](screens/R09.md): `question-library-archive-confirmation-open--archive-confirmation`, mobile bottom selection bar. “selected” and “Cancel” break inside narrow controls. | A consequential choice receives less readable treatment than ordinary browsing. | Major / high | Provide stable readable count, consequence, cancel and confirm roles at 390px and smaller target widths. |
| F04 · Large subject inventory / distant editor | [R27](screens/R27.md): 273-subject default inventory; mobile original 54,774px high, unsaved-editor view 56,736px. ## Preserve deliberately

- **Learning character:** public hero/subject art, Plant Lab’s field-notebook world, Current Lab’s meaningful simulation and guided sequence.
- **Trustworthy state language:** paper status/action distinctions, saved/unchanged paper semantics and realistic scheduling expectations.
- **Continuity:** quiz answers retained through an offline save failure, explicit retry and saved confirmation; voluntary invitation return cue and manual clipboard fallback.
- **Admin safeguards:** loaded/awaiting counts, selection scope, flagged rows excluded from approval, review reasons, source/completeness requirements and confirmation before archive/rejection.

Each retained behavior has a bounded evidence trail in [journeys.md](journeys.md). Presence in a screenshot alone is not treated as a successful outcome.

## Evidence questions to resolve at the appropriate gate

| Question | Why it remains open | Resolution required |
|---|---|---|

| Does Plant Lab’s sticky scene obscure the instruction during actual mobile use? | The fertilisation full-page image has overlap; source confirms sticky scene and action bar. Do not call this an always-present functional failure yet. |
| Do real legacy paper records contain usable identity? | R03 has near-empty rows and an incomplete-update message; fixtures target the newer paper model. | Check representative legacy records and route relevance. Retire or redesign only after the route’s role is established. |
| Is a needs-info question available beside the answer field? | R08’s fixture asks the user to look in activity; actual clarification rendering depends on seeded activity/model details. | Use a representative actual pending-clarification record before finalizing that state. |
| Do end-to-end continuity and recovery hold? | Admin submissions, lab saved visits, joining/hosting transitions and growing-history retrieval were outside the executed capture actions. | Prototype/implementation task checks for the affected journey; retain null scores until observed. |
| Are accessibility and motion requirements met? | The baseline used reduced motion and animation-disabled stills, not a conformance/performance study. | Validate keyboard/focus, contrast, zoom, long text, touch targets, reduced-motion parity and device/browser behavior in the interactive stage. |

These questions limit the corresponding claims. They do not erase the available screen evidence or require repeating the complete baseline capture.
