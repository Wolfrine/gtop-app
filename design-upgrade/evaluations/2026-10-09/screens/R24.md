## Observations

**Preserve:** Readable questions, expandable answer/explanation and local editing access.

**Finding (moderate, high confidence):** The JSON editor changes the task from question maintenance into raw syntax editing. In a valid empty curriculum scope the message still says “Select filters to view MCQs”; the selected filters are collapsed, obscuring the distinction.

**Improvement objective:** Keep scope visible and distinguish no selection from no matches; use question content and validation as the editing focus.
