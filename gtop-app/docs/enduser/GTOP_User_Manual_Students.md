# GTOP User Manual — Students

## 1. What is GTOP?
- Growth Tutorials Objective Practice (GTOP) is a quiz app for classes 5–12.
- You answer MCQs mapped to your board, standard, subject, and chapter.
- You can practice solo, build your own quiz, or join real-time sessions (Live Quiz or Quiz Run).

## 2. Sign in and set up your profile
1. Go to the GTOP site (homepage shows the hero banner and sample MCQ).
2. Click **Start for Free** or **Sign in with Google**.
3. Choose your Google account to continue.
4. Complete profile setup:
   - Pick your **Standard** and **Board** from the dropdowns.
   - Press **Continue** to save and go to your dashboard.
5. You can switch accounts anytime from the profile setup page.

## 3. Where to start a quiz
- **Quick Quiz:** From the dashboard, click **Quick Quiz** to get ~10 random MCQs from your syllabus.
- **Quiz Builder:** From the dashboard, click **Quiz Builder** to pick subjects/chapters and question count. Save starts a custom quiz with those filters.
- **Quiz Run Lobby:** From the dashboard, open **Quiz Run Lobby** to host or join a scheduled race-style quiz.
- **Upcoming Live Quiz:** From the dashboard, open **Upcoming Live Quiz** to browse or join live, teacher-hosted sessions.

## 4. Taking a quiz (solo/custom)
1. Each question shows the board, standard, subject, and chapter.
2. Tap an option card (A–D). It highlights as selected.
3. Use **Next** to move forward. On the last question, the button becomes **Submit**.
4. There is no timer in normal mode; answer at your own pace.

## 5. Taking part in Live Quiz or Quiz Run
- Live/Run attempts open the same quiz screen but with extras:
  - A circular timer counts down per question.
  - Once you pick an option, it freezes for that question.
  - A mini leaderboard shows who answered fastest for the current item.
  - When time runs out or the host ends the session, you go to summary automatically.

## 6. Reviewing your results
- After any quiz you land on **Quiz Summary**:
  - Donut chart with accuracy % and quick chips for total and correct answers.
  - Buttons: **Retry Incorrect**, **Retry All**, and **Back to Dashboard**.
  - Toggle **Show only incorrect** to focus on mistakes.
  - Expand each question to see your choice vs. the correct answer. If available, an explanation appears under the correct option.
  - Use the flag icon to report a bad question (requires sign-in).
  - Live/Run attempts also show the live leaderboard inside the summary.

## 7. Reports & analytics
1. Open **View Reports** from the dashboard or go to **/reports**.
2. The page shows:
   - **Summary cards** for total quizzes, average accuracy, and last score.
   - **Accuracy Over Time** line chart (per day) and **Quiz Type Distribution** pie chart.
   - **Session History** list: each row shows quiz type, date/time, board/standard path, score, and a progress bar.
3. Click a session to reopen its detailed summary.
4. Use **Retry Incorrect** or **Retry Entire Quiz** from each session to practice again.

## 9. Tips for parents and students
- Use Quiz Builder to target weak chapters.
- Check **Accuracy Over Time** weekly to see improvements.
- For live events, join a stable network and answer within the per-question timer.
