# Student-submitted handwritten answer sheets

## Source

GT Papers owner decision, 23 November 2026. The owner requested student uploads in GTOP and approved the proposed workflow.

## Context

Teachers currently receive scanned scripts individually, save the files, find the student, and add the PDF to GT Papers. A student can use the GTOP account they already have and send their scans directly.

## Decisions

- When GT supplies a test link, the student uploads the answer sheet. For an external or unlisted paper, the student submits the question paper and answers separately.
- GT Papers groups incoming work by confirmed question paper and version. Staff approve ready submissions as they arrive and send back only work that needs corrections.
- Staff approval starts checking. Staff review and release the annotated result privately to the signed-in student.
- Pilot both sources of question papers and measure teacher time, rescans, turnaround, and agreement with teacher judgments.
