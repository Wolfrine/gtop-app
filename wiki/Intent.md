# Intent

## Summary

GTOP exists to make Growth Tutorials practice easier to start, easier to repeat, and easier to understand. It turns curriculum-aligned MCQs into a product experience where students can practice independently, join live quiz events, review performance, and see what their next useful learning move should be.

## Why It Matters

For students, GTOP should reduce the friction between "I should practice" and "I completed a useful quiz and know what to improve next." Students are already putting effort into preparation; the product should help them see where that effort is working, where it is not, and how to improve weak topics without depending only on a teacher noticing the gap.

For Growth Tutorials, GTOP is a dedicated objective-practice product surface that can evolve independently while still supporting broader ecosystem flows. It should protect the credibility of Growth Tutorials by making practice content trustworthy and by turning usage data into learning guidance.

## Current Understanding

The product is organized around board, standard, subject, and chapter context. That structure lets GTOP serve focused practice instead of generic question drills. The current experience includes quick quizzes, custom quizzes, reports, live quiz sessions, quiz runs, and admin tools for keeping the question bank and syllabus useful.

GTOP's product direction is not just "store MCQs." The intent is to create a repeatable practice loop: choose relevant practice, answer questions, see results, retry weak areas, and use live or group modes to add energy to learning.

Recent product discussion strengthens this direction: question-bank improvement should support the broader learning product, not become the whole product. The current question bank is large enough that verification and enrichment are urgent, but the strategic reason is bigger than cleanup. GTOP needs reliable content because reliable content is the base for diagnosis, topic feedback, student trust, and future AI-assisted learning.

AI-assisted learning support is a durable direction when it helps students understand strong areas, weak areas, daily targets, and alternative explanations for difficult topics. That direction depends on trustworthy quiz content, richer question metadata, and enough student activity to make recommendations meaningful.

The long-term product direction is a future-ready practice system: disciplined MCQ practice, richer question metadata, student-specific feedback, and AI used where it improves learning clarity. AI should not be treated as decorative novelty. It should help classify questions, identify patterns, explain weak topics in multiple ways, and support concise feedback that a student can act on.
