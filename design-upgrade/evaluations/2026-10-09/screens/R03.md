## Observations

**Preserve:** Explicit incomplete-update notice and separate in-progress/saved groups.

**Finding (major, medium confidence):** In the captured legacy state, 16 repeated rows expose action buttons but almost no identifying text; one queued badge is visible. A user cannot distinguish the records from this view.

**Improvement objective:** Establish whether this legacy route remains useful; if retained, make each record identifiable before its action.

**Evidence limit / follow-up:** The fixture was seeded for the current paper model and the screen says results may be incomplete. This is a captured-state assessment, not proof that all real legacy records are blank. Verify representative legacy data before implementation.
