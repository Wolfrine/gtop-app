## Observations

**Preserve:** Prominent join code, participant count and distinct host/guest next-step wording.

**Finding (major, medium confidence):** Participant icons are detached above names rather than aligned in a compact row. The missing-session fixture renders an ordinary waiting lobby with no code instead of an explicit missing-session explanation.

**Improvement objective:** Make waiting, readiness and invalid sessions visibly distinct; align participant identity and role consistently.

**Evidence limit / follow-up:** Missing-ID rendering is observed in the emulator. End-to-end joining, host start, reconnection and session expiry were not exercised.
