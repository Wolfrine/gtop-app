# Intent

## Summary

GTOP exists to make Growth Tutorials practice easier to start, easier to repeat, and easier to understand. It turns curriculum-aligned MCQs into a product experience where students can practice independently, join live events, review performance, and see what their next useful learning move should be.

## Why It Matters

For students, GTOP should reduce the friction between "I should practice" and "I completed a useful quiz and know what to improve next." Students are already putting effort into preparation; the product should help them see where that effort is working, where it is not, and how to improve weak topics without depending only on a teacher noticing the gap.

For Growth Tutorials, GTOP is a dedicated objective-practice product surface that can evolve independently while still supporting broader ecosystem flows. It should protect brand credibility through [[Question Bank Quality]] and through feedback that makes practice feel worth repeating.

## Current Understanding

The product is organized around board, standard, subject, and chapter context. That structure lets GTOP serve focused practice instead of generic question drills. The current experience includes quick quizzes, custom quizzes, reports, [[Live Practice]], and admin tools for keeping the question bank and syllabus useful.

GTOP's intent is not just to store MCQs. The intent is to build a repeatable [[Practice Loop]] supported by trusted content, useful review, and clearer next-practice guidance. That loop is the base for [[Learning Diagnosis]] and future AI-assisted feedback.

The long-term [[Product Direction]] is a future-ready practice system: disciplined MCQ practice, richer question metadata, student-specific feedback, and AI used where it improves learning clarity. AI should not be treated as decorative novelty. It should help classify questions, identify patterns, explain weak topics in multiple ways, and support concise feedback that a student can act on.

## Related Product Concepts

The 24 October 2026 direction preserves objective practice as GTOP core and gives Question Paper Creation its own conversational sub-app, beginning with Aparna. The assistant should understand and clarify naturally in English, Hindi and Hinglish, retain relevant context, and stay responsive while substantial content work runs. Original requests and corrections remain authoritative through delegation. Actual progress, dependable saved outputs and usable revisions matter more than an impressive model demonstration. Improve the working journey through observed use and feedback; a central launcher and wider capabilities follow later decisions.

Question Paper Creation is intended to serve Classes 5–12 across GTOP's configured boards and the appropriate programmes. Class 12 Chemistry was a testing example, not the assistant's product boundary. The assistant speaks for the complete service: it can discuss requirements, arrange reviewed papers and separate answers, revise earlier work, explain questions and report actual progress. It should reuse saved GTOP curricula and check official current sources when requested. Saved chapters alone do not establish current-year completeness; programme, level, examination cohort and textbook differences must be clarified rather than silently selected.

The assistant should be short, sweet and friendly: answer the current question, accept clear requirements and remember details already given. Detailed explanations belong where requested or necessary for the task. Daily conversation reviews should produce evidence and a proposed correction plan for Sachit's confirmation, rather than silently changing behavior. Administrators should be able to find existing papers by board, standard and subject and read their linked discussions, helping them reuse work before requesting another paper. Conversation titles should describe the completed paper.

Settled requirements should survive the conversation. When an administrator supplies chapters and says “all topics,” the scope is every topic within those chapters; the assistant should not repeatedly demand a narrower topic or expand it to the whole subject. Ask only for a genuine remaining gap, preserve corrections through delegation, and describe actual execution honestly. Discussing a board does not select it, and pausing creation must remain effective until the administrator asks to proceed. A useful harness should help the local model meet these expectations without making teachers manage the model's limitations.

The paper workspace should be calm and compact. Teachers must be able to read the discussion, understand current progress and find the next paper action without oversized chat text, repeated guidance or wasted space. Filters, feedback and earlier revisions remain easy to reach when needed. Question Paper Creation has its own recognizable, installable identity while retaining Growth Tutorials' private sign-in. Installation offers convenient access; conversation and paper creation still depend on connectivity. Agent review is supporting evidence, and the intended users' experience remains the acceptance standard.

Clear paper requirements should reach Codex promptly. Local difficulty should lead to delegation after one or two turns, preserving original intent instead of repeating questions. The assistant should improve through source-backed memory, validated examples and reviewed corrections from use. Learning does not confer permission or guarantee educational accuracy; the controller remains responsible for authority and dependable delivery.

GTOP's [[Features]] should serve student practice continuity, admin content quality, and Growth Tutorials brand trust. The [[Problems]] page tracks the product problems that should shape future priorities.
