# Intent

## Summary

GTOP exists to make Growth Tutorials practice easier to start, easier to repeat, and easier to understand. It turns curriculum-aligned MCQs into a product experience where students can practice independently, join live events, review performance, and see what their next useful learning move should be.

## Why It Matters

For students, GTOP should reduce the friction between "I should practice" and "I completed a useful quiz and know what to improve next." Students are already putting effort into preparation; the product should help them see where that effort is working, where it is not, and how to improve weak topics without depending only on a teacher noticing the gap.

For Growth Tutorials, GTOP is a dedicated objective-practice product surface that can evolve independently while still supporting broader ecosystem flows. It should protect brand credibility through [[Question Bank Quality]] and through feedback that makes practice feel worth repeating.

## Current Understanding

The product is organized around board, standard, subject, and chapter context. That structure lets GTOP serve focused practice instead of generic question drills. The current experience includes quick quizzes, custom quizzes, reports, [[Live Practice]], and admin tools for keeping the question bank and syllabus useful.

GTOP's intent is not just to store MCQs. The intent is to build a repeatable [[Practice Loop]] supported by trusted content, useful review, and clearer next-practice guidance. That loop is the base for [[Learning Diagnosis]] and future AI-assisted feedback.

The long-term [[Product Direction]] is a future-ready practice system: disciplined MCQ practice, richer question metadata, student-specific feedback, and AI used where it improves learning clarity. AI should not be treated as decorative novelty. It should help classify questions, identify patterns, explain weak topics in multiple ways, and support concise feedback that a student can act on.
