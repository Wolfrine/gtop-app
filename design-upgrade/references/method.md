# Step 3 method

Authorized 9 October 2026. Research only; no GTOP screen ratings, mockups or application changes.

## Selection
Screen a varied discovery set, then retain 6–8 references with inspectable evidence. Include expressive brand experiences, learning interactions, and useful product/dense-interface examples. Award status is a discovery signal, not a proxy for suitability or student preference. Older teaching examples are mechanism references, not claims about current trends.

## Evidence
For selected references preserve canonical URL, source attribution, capture date, desktop 1440×1000 and mobile-emulated 390×844 originals, normal-motion video, action timestamps, reverse/fast/slow scrolling and a reduced-motion preference check. Keep failed and incomplete captures as diagnostics. Inspect controls in a separate targeted pass. A record being generated does not mean it is acceptable evidence.

The established isolated public-capture workflow saves originals to this repository. Browser inspection and keyframe review supply evaluation; do not infer smoothness or exact animation durations from stills. Capture timestamps include automation/processing overhead. Desktop video is scaled to 960×667; PNG screenshots remain at the full viewport resolution. Mobile emulation is not a physical phone. No account creation, purchases or private-reference data are needed.

## Analysis
Record composition, typography, colour/light, image/material treatment, density/rhythm, motion character, identity/cohesion and emotional interpretation. Study relationships and task suitability, not a checklist of decorative ingredients.

Each motion finding follows: user action → observed change → attention direction → intended feeling (hypothesis) → GTOP applicability → limitations. Separate observed facts, evaluator judgments, creator statements, expert recognition and real-user reactions. No recruited student panel exists for this study; do not claim a universal definition of beauty or measured learning benefit.

## Completion gate
Retain evidence-backed reference dossiers and a comparison report; explain weaknesses and non-transferable patterns. Calibrate the existing rubric without silently changing its weights or thresholds. Missing behavior remains unscored. Step 4 begins only after this reference work is reviewed as being on track. Action Plan remains excluded under D-008.
