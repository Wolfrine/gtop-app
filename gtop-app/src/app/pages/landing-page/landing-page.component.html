BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few things
to know.

Growth Tutorials
Objective Practice.

Who is GTOP for?+

Learners in classes 5–12. Choose your board and class to find the subjects and chapters available for you.

How do I start practising?+

Sign in with Google and set up your board and class. Start a Quick Quiz or choose your own subjects and chapters in a Custom Quiz.

Can I review my answers?+

Yes. Review your answers after a quiz, see previous attempts and retry incorrect questions.

Can I practise with friends?+

Yes. Create a Quiz Run and share the join code, or join a Live Quiz organised by a host. Participants sign in to take part.
