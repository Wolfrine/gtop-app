# Raw Note - 2026-09-05 - Experience Quality Target

## Source

Founder feedback after the V1 standardisation rollout and subsequent discussion in this chat.

## Context

The founder found the result more aligned and structured but plain, placid and unattractive. The discussion established that the previous work had concrete operational objectives but no demanding definition of the finished visual quality. Retrospective ratings had incorrectly been treated as if they were original targets.

## Key Points

- Attractive, distinctive and inviting learning experiences are explicit objectives, alongside clarity, usability and reliability.
- Define the quality bar before work and use the same criteria to assess the result.
- Preserve V1 functionality while developing a stronger visual direction using actual content.
- Demonstrate the dashboard, a quiz question and results on desktop and mobile before extending the design.
- The founder chose to continue the full process in this conversation and authorized the work with "Okay go ahead".
- The founder's quality judgment and actual learner response remain distinct from the agent's review scores.

## Decisions

- Treat visual quality and product personality as first-class outcomes, not assumed consequences of consistency.
- Present a concrete experience preview for review before a wider production rollout.
- Keep objectives, rationale and evidence in the owning repository.

## Open Questions

- Does the proposed direction meet the founder's expectation of an attractive learning product?
- How do learners respond to the redesigned experience after release?
