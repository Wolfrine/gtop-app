## Observations

**Preserve:** Session status, participants, per-question submissions and host controls are present.

**Finding (major, high confidence):** In both mobile lobby and active captures, long question titles overlap adjacent expansion rows and submission chips. This is a concrete readability failure, independent of the seeded timer values.

**Improvement objective:** Keep the active question, submission count and host action readable at mobile width; rows must grow with real text.
