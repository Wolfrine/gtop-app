# Intent

## Summary

GTOP exists to make Growth Tutorials practice easier to start, easier to repeat, and easier to review. It turns curriculum-aligned MCQs into a product experience where students can practice independently, join live quiz events, review performance, and retry weak areas.

## Why It Matters

For students, GTOP should reduce the friction between "I should practice" and "I completed a useful quiz and know what to improve next." For Growth Tutorials, it provides a dedicated objective-practice product surface that can evolve independently while still supporting broader ecosystem flows.

## Current Understanding

The product is organized around board, standard, subject, and chapter context. That structure lets GTOP serve focused practice instead of generic question drills. The current experience includes quick quizzes, custom quizzes, reports, live quiz sessions, quiz runs, and admin tools for keeping the question bank and syllabus useful.

GTOP's product direction is not just "store MCQs." The intent is to create a repeatable practice loop: choose relevant practice, answer questions, see results, retry weak areas, and use live or group modes to add energy to learning.
