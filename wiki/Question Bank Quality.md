# Question Bank Quality

## Definition

Question-bank quality is the degree to which GTOP's objective and written-question banks are correct, syllabus-aligned, discoverable, well-covered, well-tagged, and continuously improved. It is the product's trust base.

Quality is not the same as volume. A large bank matters only when students can reliably practice the right curriculum area and when reports, retry flows, and diagnosis are built on questions that deserve trust.

## Why It Matters

GTOP's most urgent product pressure is that weak content quality weakens everything above it. Reports become questionable, [[Learning Diagnosis]] becomes noisy, [[Student Pulse]] overclaims, and Growth Tutorials brand credibility suffers.

The founder discussion treats question-bank work as a major ongoing activity, not a one-time cleanup. The product memory should preserve that intensity: GTOP needs both addition of missing questions and improvement of existing questions as a durable capability.

## Current GTOP State

GTOP already has a broad MCQ surface organized by board, standard, subject, and chapter, plus learner flagging and admin content tools. Public copy references 50,000+ board-aligned questions and refreshed content, while product discussion references roughly 1.2 lakh existing questions needing verification. That mismatch is a product claim to reconcile, not a number for the wiki to resolve.

Current product signals point toward the next quality layer: topic-level tags, question type, question pattern, difficulty, verification state, source/status, coverage gaps, repeated wrong-answer patterns, and flag resolution.

Today, quality means students can usually find relevant board-aligned questions and report problems. The future quality bar is higher: questions should be verified, tagged deeply enough to support diagnosis, monitored for repeated confusion, and improved through an ongoing admin review rhythm.

## Quality Dimensions

Durable question-bank quality should include:

- Correct answer and solution trust.
- Coverage across board, standard, subject, chapter, and topic.
- Metadata that supports diagnosis, including type, pattern, topic, difficulty, and status.
- Learner flag signals that feed admin review.
- Ongoing enrichment of missing, weak, stale, or poorly tagged content.

## Admin Content Quality

Admins and teachers are operators of question-bank trust. Their role is not only to add MCQs; it is to maintain content health so the student experience remains credible.

Admin content-quality surfaces should help decide what needs attention first: flagged questions, coverage gaps, stale items, missing topic tags, repeated wrong-answer patterns, or unverified high-use questions. The exact workflow and prioritization remain open product questions.

Example snapshot: if several students flag the same MCQ or repeatedly choose the same wrong option, an admin review should treat it as a trust signal. The outcome may be confirming the answer, improving the explanation, correcting tags, replacing the question, or marking the item as verified.

## One Admin Journey

The24October2026 decision starts Aparna's private conversational Question Paper sub-app and keeps the previous creator stopped. Preserve existing papers/data while changing the experience. The older bank-first and scheduled-service paragraphs below describe the previous journey; the new pilot uses original checked practice questions and separate worked answers, without claiming live-bank access. Teacher review remains required. Natural conversation, actual progress, preserved intent and immutable revisions are part of content trust. See [[Decisions]].

Admins should express a paper requirement in natural language and return through the same stable paper record, with the request conversation and completed outputs connected and all saved work easy to find. The founder identifies mobile complexity, vague requirement discovery and inconsistent entry points as real operating problems. Simplicity must cover the full lifecycle: requirements, submission, scheduled waiting, preparation, clarification, completion, revision, cancellation and later retrieval. The next action should be clear without making an admin rediscover the process on every visit.

A paper needs a stable identity connecting its requirements, progress, clarification and outputs. Submitting a request must leave a discoverable record and status. Returning after years, with thousands of papers in the history, must still make it practical to find the intended paper and continue from the right point; an ever-growing dropdown does not meet that expectation. Dependable saved work and responsive return visits are part of this product requirement.

Chapter-wise written-question management belongs alongside objective content. Teachers need to find, add, correct and retire those questions independently of an active generation request. A written answer's depth and marks must fit the paper section; available content must not be relabelled to conceal a shortage. Saved papers should preserve the exact delivered questions and a separate answer key. A revision creates a new version and keeps the last completed result available while its replacement is pending.

The founder replaced the earlier manual question-approval gate for chat papers with automatic generation and delivery. The agent checks suitable existing questions first and creates only what is missing, preserving scope, type, marks and difficulty. The chosen service rhythm is every two hours from 8 AM to 8 PM India time. Waiting for a scheduled run, active preparation, a need for clarification, completion, cancellation and inability to complete must be distinguishable. Cancelling or revising pending work must have predictable consequences. Automatic delivery still requires checked answers and usable diagrams. New question flagging is a later option, and chat-paper delivery does not by itself publish content to learners.

Service labels must reflect actual capabilities. All admins have access; creator attribution permanently records who created the paper and is not an assignable owner. The current notification channels are in-app and opt-in push. Current outputs are HTML, with Print / Save as PDF supplied through the browser. These facts should remain clear when describing who can act, how an admin will hear about a result and what can be printed or saved.

Private activity evidence should help improve this journey: which areas and actions admins use, where operations fail, and approximate active time. It should not create a new reporting task for admins, collect their typed content, or turn activity duration into a productivity ranking. At most a monthly readout is part of the chosen operating routine. A complete returning-admin journey at realistic history volume is the review unit; actual usability and workload improvements remain to be established through usage and feedback.

## Current-Year Curriculum Trust

The founder wants complete current-year syllabus coverage across boards, standards, subjects and chapters, with administrators able to preview official sources, correct omissions and approve what is accurate. This is part of question-bank trust: existing question coverage cannot define the syllabus, because missing questions would then hide missing teaching content.

A full-syllabus paper should mean the applicable approved curriculum, including its year or examination cohort and assessment scope. Imported content remains provisional until reviewed; an incomplete source inventory must stay visibly incomplete. Later corrections should preserve what earlier papers were based on. These expectations extend [[Decisions]] without claiming that a comprehensive catalogue has already been verified.

Teachers must be able to request chapters absent from the question bank. Such a request expresses intended coverage and exposes a content gap; it does not make unverified generated questions ready for learners.

## Boundaries And Non-Goals

This page should not define database fields, moderation states, scripts, or dashboard tasks. It preserves the product model for quality. Execution belongs to docs, implementation plans, or the project manager agent.

Question-bank quality also should not be used to delay every student-facing improvement. GTOP can improve review and practice now, but any strong personalization claim must be proportional to content trust.

## Relationship To Other Concepts

[[Practice Loop]] depends on relevant and trustworthy questions. [[Learning Diagnosis]] depends on accurate metadata and enough verified attempts. [[Student Pulse]] should stay conservative until question quality and usage evidence are strong. [[Product Direction]] treats question-bank quality as the foundation for practice intelligence.

## Decision Guidance

When prioritizing product work, question-bank improvements should be favored when they unlock trust, coverage, diagnosis, or repeat usage. The strongest candidates are improvements that affect many student attempts or reduce uncertainty in feedback.
